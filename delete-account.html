账号与数据删除（众生佛）

本页说明如何删除「众生佛」（Zhongshengfo）应用的账号及相关数据。

在应用内删除账号

  1. 打开「众生佛」App 并登录；
  2. 进入「我的」→「设置」；
  3. 点击「注销账号」，阅读提示后点击「申请注销」并确认。

无法打开应用时

请用注册手机号发送邮件至 461812195@qq.com，标题注明「删除账号」，我们会在 15 个工作日内核实身份并处理。

将被删除的数据

账号、手机号、昵称与头像、打卡与修行记录、收藏与播放记录，以及您发布的动态与评论。账号注销后数据无法恢复。

可能保留的数据

依法律法规要求保留的交易与支付凭证（如应用商店订单记录）会在法定期限内保留，其余数据在处理完成后删除或匿名化。

Delete your account and data (Zhongshengfo)

This page explains how to delete your account and data for the "众生佛 (Zhongshengfo)" app.

In the app

  1. Open the app and sign in;
  2. Go to Me → Settings;
  3. Tap "Delete Account" (注销账号), then confirm.

If you cannot open the app

Email 461812195@qq.com from your registered contact with the subject "Delete account". We will verify and process the request within 15 business days.

Data deleted

Your account, phone number, nickname and avatar, check-in and practice records, favorites and playback history, and posts/comments you published. Deletion is permanent.

Data that may be retained

Transaction records required by law (e.g. app store order records) are retained for the legally required period; all other data is deleted or anonymized after processing.
